# The standard

What every carousel and video from this kit follows. The generator enforces the measurable parts and prints a "Standards check".

## Structure
- **5–9 slides.** Cover, 3–6 points, one call to action.
- **Cover hook ≤ 12 words.** Name the payoff, not the topic.
- **One idea per slide, ≤ 40 words.** Headline first, support line second.
- **Last slide asks for one thing** (save, comment a keyword, share), never three.

## Type
- Headline in a serif; supporting text in a clean sans. Never more than two families per slide.
- Mix roman and *italic* in a headline for emphasis; one highlighted word per slide at most.
- Tracked small caps for labels, brand and kickers.
- If text had to shrink below ~60% to fit, the copy is too long. Cut it, don't shrink it.

## Photography
- **One photo per slide**, full-bleed, at least 1080 px wide (2000 px+ is better).
- Prefer real light, texture and negative space where type will sit.
- Light photos get dark type with a light wash; dark photos get light type with a dark wash. The generator reads each photo's brightness and chooses.
- Keep faces and key subjects out of the zone behind the headline.

## Colour and finish
- Palette comes from `brand.json`: one accent, a light, a dark. Don't add colours per slide.
- Film grain on every slide for a printed, editorial feel.

## Video (9:16)
- Keep text inside the safe zone (the renderer pads ~250 px top and ~340 px bottom for platform UI).
- 2.5–6 s per scene; text animates in word by word; fade between scenes.

## Voice
- Direct, specific, a little dry. No filler openers ("In today's world…"), no emoji clusters, no hashtag walls on slides.
